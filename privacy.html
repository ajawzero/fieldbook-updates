Fieldbook for iPhone and iPad: Privacy

Effective November 10, 2026

Fieldbook records your meetings and keeps your notes on your device. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It never sends anything to us, and it doesn't send anything to a server on its own. We don't collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Recordings, notes, the names you mark, and attachments are saved in Fieldbook on your iPhone or iPad.
  • The iPhone and iPad app has no AI features. It doesn't transcribe, and it makes no requests to AI services.
  • Delete a recording in Fieldbook, or delete the app, and it's gone from the device.

What leaves your device, and only when you choose

  • Send to Computer: a recording goes to Fieldbook on your own Mac or PC on the same network. Or the share sheet sends it by AirDrop to your Mac, or to any app you pick there. It includes the audio, your notes, the names you marked, and attachments. With Use Calendar on, it also includes the meeting's title, time, and the invitees' names.
  • Sync with your Mac: once you pair the device with Fieldbook on your Mac, they sync over your local network or a direct Wi-Fi link while Fieldbook is open on both. Your recordings go to the Mac on each sync. The Mac's recent meetings come to the device to read, with their transcripts and notes, along with its customer accounts, its calendar for the next few days, and its folder rules (including the email domains that file meetings).
  • Share Diagnostic Log: if you share it, it holds the app's activity log and a summary. The log records what the app did and when, including folder names, the email domains that filed recordings, the local network addresses used to reach your computer, audio device names, and errors. The summary has the app and iOS versions, the device model, and each recording's title, time, length, and status. It never includes audio, notes, or attachments.

On your computer, Fieldbook transcribes recordings itself. If you set up an AI service in Fieldbook there, it sends transcript text to that service to write notes. That's your choice and your service's own terms.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record meetings you start. While it records, Fieldbook shows its recording bar and Lock Screen card, and iOS shows its microphone indicator.
  • Camera: only to scan the code Fieldbook on your computer shows, to send to it or to pair with your Mac. No pictures are kept.
  • Calendar (optional, off until you turn on Use Calendar): to name a recording after the meeting happening now.
  • Local network: to find your own computer, to send recordings, and to sync with your paired Mac.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations differ by place. Tell everyone in the meeting that you're recording, and get their consent where the law requires it.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test Fieldbook through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares the name and email you were invited with, your device and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback you send with us, under Apple's own privacy policy. We use them only to fix and improve Fieldbook.

Children

Fieldbook is a tool for work meetings and isn't made for children.
